Fund Summary & Settings

Every Actual Presentation Day = 100% of that day's collection:
• 50% Performance Attendance: that day's 50% is divided equally ONLY among participants marked PRESENT on that date.
• 15% Choir Fund: that day's 15% is deducted from the collection and reserved for the Choir Fund; it is NOT added to any participant's personal share.
• 15% Sacristan Fund: that day's 15% is deducted from the collection and reserved for the Sacristan Fund; it is NOT added to any participant's personal share.
• 20% Rehearsal Share: that day's 20% is added to the accumulated Rehearsal Fund. The accumulated pool is divided across the number of rehearsals you set; each rehearsal's portion is shared equally ONLY among those marked PRESENT at that rehearsal. Absences mean no share for that session.

Each day's four portions add up to exactly 100%. Daily presentation portions are calculated independently; the 20% rehearsal portions accumulate across performance days. The 30% ministry funds are set aside and are NOT part of the individual payout. The reconciliation counts them as reserved funds, not participant shares.
